## Datasets

The three course tables. Each help page says what one row is, the units,
the source, the vintage and what was changed.

- [`weo`](https://teal-insights.github.io/saisdata/reference/weo.md) :
  World Economic Outlook, October 2026: the tidy country panel
- [`weo_groups`](https://teal-insights.github.io/saisdata/reference/weo_groups.md)
  : World Economic Outlook, October 2026: the two big groups, as the IMF
  lays them out
- [`wb_groups`](https://teal-insights.github.io/saisdata/reference/wb_groups.md)
  : The World Bank's country groups: region, income level and lending
  type
