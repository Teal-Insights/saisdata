weo                  package:saisdata                  R Documentation

World Economic Outlook, October 2026: the tidy country panel

Description:

     Four indicators from the IMF's World Economic Outlook (WEO)
     database of October 2026, laid out tidy. One row is one entity in
     one year, and each variable has its own column. An entity is one
     of 197 countries or one of 13 IMF country groups, and the years
     run from 2000 to 2031, so the panel has 6,720 rows.

Usage:

     weo

Format:

     A tibble with 6,720 rows and 9 variables:

     code chr The entity's code. A country has its three-letter ISO
          code (the IMF uses KOS for Kosovo and WBG for West Bank
          and Gaza); an IMF group has a code that starts with G and a
          digit or an X, such as G110 for Advanced Economies.

     name chr The entity's name, as the IMF writes it, such as "Egypt,
          Arab Republic of".

     year int The year, 2000 to 2031.

     gross_debt dbl Gross debt of the general government, everything it
          owes, in percent of GDP (the IMF's GGXWDG_NGDP).

     primary_balance dbl The primary balance: revenue minus spending,
          with interest left out on both sides, in percent of GDP
          (GGXONLB_NGDP). Negative means a deficit.

     overall_balance dbl The overall balance: revenue minus all
          spending, interest included, in percent of GDP
          (GGXCNL_NGDP). Negative means a deficit.

     gdp_usd_bn dbl GDP at current prices, in billions of US dollars
          (NGDPD).

     latest_actual int The last year with actual fiscal data; later
          years are IMF estimates or projections. Missing (NA) for 12
          of the 13 IMF groups.

     fiscal_year chr The months of the year the WEO shows the fiscal
          numbers in: "January/December" for most countries, and others
          such as "July/June" for Pakistan. Missing for 12 of the 13
          IMF groups.

Details:

     The WEO mixes actual data and IMF projections in one row.
     latest_actual marks where the actual data stop. For most
     countries the fiscal numbers are actual through 2025, and from
     2026 on almost every value is an IMF projection.

     Net interest is not a column. It is one line of arithmetic,
     primary_balance - overall_balance: the interest the government
     pays minus the interest it receives, in percent of GDP. For Japan
     in 2025 that is -0.511 minus -0.709, which is 0.198.

     To tell a group from a country, look at the code. An IMF group's
     code starts with G and then a digit or an X (G110); a country's
     second character is a letter (Ghana is GHA).

Vintage:

     The IMF's World Economic Outlook database of October 2026. This
     table is a frozen snapshot of that release: it does not change
     when the IMF publishes a new WEO or revises these numbers.

Transformations:

     The IMF's workbook WEOOct2026all.xlsx went through two steps.
     First, an extract stacked its Countries and Country Groups sheets,
     kept 13 of 44 indicators, the years 2000 to 2031 and 9 of 29
     descriptive columns, and wrote each number in its shortest form.
     Second, data-raw/weo_panel.R kept the four indicators here, gave
     them plain names, reshaped them to one row per entity and year,
     and rounded them to three decimals. It also added two columns from
     the IMF's labels. fiscal_year is the IMF's own label.
     latest_actual turns the IMF's label, such as FY2024/25, into one
     calendar year, following the rule in the IMF's notes for that
     country.

Description:

     The two groups, Advanced Economies and Emerging Market and
     Developing Economies, together cover the world. The three
     indicators, the primary balance, the overall balance and GDP, are
     all you need to work out what the world's governments pay in
     interest.

Usage:

     weo_groups

Format:

     A tibble with 6 rows and 36 variables:

     group_code chr The IMF's code for the group: G110 for Advanced
          Economies, G200 for Emerging Market and Developing
          Economies (COUNTRY.ID in the IMF's file).

     group chr The group's name, as the IMF writes it (COUNTRY).

     indicator chr The IMF's code for the indicator (INDICATOR.ID):
          GGXONLB_NGDP is the primary balance, GGXCNL_NGDP the
          overall balance, and NGDPD GDP at current prices.

     indicator_name chr The indicator's full name, as the IMF writes it
          (INDICATOR), such as "Net lending (+) / net borrowing (-),
          General government, Percent of GDP".

     units chr How to read the numbers: "Percent of GDP" for the two
          balances and "Billions of US dollars" for GDP (the IMF's
          SCALE and UNIT columns, in plain words).

     2001 to 2031 dbl One column per year, holding the indicator's
          value that year. From 2026 on the values are IMF projections.
          The names are numbers, so wrap them in backticks when you use
          them: `2025`.

Details:

     This is what data look like in the wild. To work with them in R
     you tidy them first: tidyr::pivot_longer() turns the year
     columns into rows, and tidyr::pivot_wider() turns the indicators
     into columns.

     Net interest in billions of US dollars is the primary balance
     minus the overall balance, times GDP, divided by 100. Added up
     over the two groups it comes to about USD 849 billion in 2001 and
     USD 2,371.2 billion in 2025. The groups' balances are averages of
     their members' balances, weighted by GDP, so this total is an
     approximation.

     balance for both groups.

The World Bank's country groups: region, income level and lending type

Description:

     The World Bank's classification of 217 countries, as its data
     service served it on 3 November 2026. One row is one country, with
     its region, its income level and the World Bank lender it borrows
     from. There are no numeric units: every column is a code or a
     label. Join it to weo by code to compare countries by income
     level or region.

Usage:

     wb_groups

Format:

     A tibble with 217 rows and 6 variables:

     code chr The country's three-letter code, the same code the IMF
          uses, so it joins to the code column of weo.

     wb_code chr The World Bank's own code. It differs from code for
          two countries only: Kosovo (XKX) and West Bank and Gaza
          (PSE).

     country chr The country's name, as the World Bank writes it, such
          as "Egypt, Arab Rep.".

     region chr The World Bank's region, one of seven, such as
          "Sub-Saharan Africa" or "South Asia".

     income_level chr The World Bank's income level: "Low income",
          "Lower middle income", "Upper middle income" or "High
          income".

     lending_type chr Which World Bank lender the country borrows from:
          "IDA" (the concessional arm, for the poorest countries),
          "IBRD", "Blend" (both), or "Not classified".

Details:

     The World Bank revises income levels every 1 July, from each
     country's gross national income per person.

     The table holds countries only, no aggregates, so a join to weo
     leaves out the IMF's group rows. 21 countries are here and not in
     the WEO, and one WEO country, Taiwan Province of China, is in the
     WEO and not here.
